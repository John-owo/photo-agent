# v0.3 culling, clustering, and shoot workflow status

Implemented:

- recursive RAW/preview indexing with relative paths, hashes, and explicit
  `high`, `ambiguous`, or `missing_preview` pairing confidence;
- optional local dimensions, capture time, camera, and lens metadata from
  preview EXIF/XMP or adjacent XMP sidecars, with per-file ingestion errors;
- exact-content duplicate groups and conservative filename-sequence burst groups;
  burst and low-resolution preview-similarity groups carry review-only rankings
  and ranking rationale;
- pluggable culling and lighting classification with `review` as the safe default;
  technical and aesthetic evidence remain separate, low-confidence rejects are
  downgraded to `review`, and configured high-value assets cannot be rejected
  automatically;
- an opt-in `OpenAIShootAnalyzer` with a schema-validated culling/lighting result,
  one shared analysis request per asset, sanitized per-session JPEG inputs, and
  mandatory `--allow-cloud-preview`; the default analyzer makes no network call;
- highest-confidence shortlisted representative selection per lighting cluster;
- durable `shoot-plan.json`, atomic job files, failure isolation, and resume that
  processes only missing/invalid jobs;
- Ctrl-C/SIGTERM-aware shoot cancellation that persists a `CANCELLED` manifest,
  pending asset IDs, and a cancellation artifact while retaining completed jobs
  for resume;
- `manifest.json`, `culling.csv`, `clusters.json`, summary counts, and concise CLI
  output;
- safe propagation-plan generation requiring an explicit global-parameter
  allowlist and explicit apply. White balance, ambiguous sources, and assets not
  in `select`/`keep` are excluded.
- a versioned baseline Parameter Registry defining normalized units, absolute
  and delta ranges, backend keys, conflict/dependency rules, confidence
  thresholds, and propagation conditions before translation or propagation;
  propagation plans carry the registry version and fail closed on unsupported
  versions or invalid operations;
- self-describing normalized plans persist the complete registry snapshot;
  unversioned, `0.1.0`, and `0.2.0` baseline plans migrate explicitly to
  registry `0.3.0`, while unsupported versions or tampered snapshots fail
  closed;
  shared translator golden-vector runners keep control-group vectors
  independently owned and compare both normalized plans and optional backend
  settings;
- Color Mixer planning covers eight channels across hue, saturation, and
  luminance with bounded absolute/delta values and conservative propagation
  thresholds. A backend must explicitly declare every requested Color Mixer
  setting before PhotoAgent can create a Workflow Copy or mutate it;
- structured Tone Curve planning uses a separate `0.1.0` registry for master,
  RGB point, and parametric variants; point endpoints/order and parametric
  bounds are schema-validated, master/parametric conflicts are explicit, and
  deterministic readback reconciliation plus independent golden vectors are
  available. Curve propagation is disabled by registry policy until per-photo
  backend readback and rendered proof exist;
- scene-aware detail planning carries required scene and ISO context, bounds
  sharpening and noise-reduction controls, validates conservative dependencies
  and duplicate requests, and provides deterministic setting/readback helpers
  plus complete capability refusal. Unsupported AI Denoise is not represented;
  detail propagation is disabled by policy until per-photo proof exists;
- context-safe optics planning separates lens correction, camera profiles, and
  geometry into bounded operations. Profile names and geometry variants use
  explicit capability allowlists, while read/current, checkpoint, and render
  prerequisites are required before a future write; broad propagation remains
  disabled by policy;
- finishing/framing planning separates vignette, grain, crop, and rotation into
  bounded controls with structured geometry readback. Crop and rotation carry
  an explicit per-photo human-review requirement, and propagation remains
  disabled until a real backend proves unrelated-state preservation;
- modern Color Grading planning is process-version scoped and keeps shadows,
  midtones, highlights, global, blending, and balance as separate control
  groups. Legacy split toning has no fallback path; requested process versions,
  control declarations, concrete settings, and read/checkpoint/render
  prerequisites are required before a future execution, while unsupported
  intent receives an explicit manual handoff;
- existing-mask planning separates read-only Master summaries from one
  verified Workflow Copy adjustment. Stable-id or unique-name selectors,
  bounded allowlisted local parameters, and deterministic preservation
  readback cover geometry, opaque fields, other masks, and global settings;
  unsupported or uncertain results become a manual handoff, and no structured
  mask mutation method is added to the current adapter;
- Style Prior planning gives protected explicit rules precedence over learned
  history, records evidence/sample count/confidence on every prior, and uses
  capped-confidence general guidance when historical samples are insufficient;
  conflicting or evidence-free rules remain review-required;
- Style Memory retrieval is reproducibly identified by dataset revision and
  SHA-256, scores scene-conditioned metadata across lighting, subject, camera,
  lens, ISO, and delivery, and reports perceptual relationships rather than
  copying raw settings. Protected natural-skin references, failed examples,
  irrelevant matches, and low-confidence history are explicitly excluded;
- held-out Style Memory evaluation freezes a shoot-level construction/held-out/
  excluded split, builds the retrieval population only from construction shoots,
  and reports population, sample size, evidence confidence, failures, and review
  outcomes without scoring construction examples against themselves;
- the PhotoAgent Bench contract freezes dataset/split identity, requires all ten
  representative photography conditions in the test split, and preserves every
  failure and `REVIEW_REQUIRED` case in the denominator. Missing outcomes become
  `REVIEW_REQUIRED`; this is an outcome/report contract, not live visual proof;
- schema-validated review files for user/Codex-supplied culling and lighting
  decisions; unknown, duplicate, and mismatched asset references fail before
  jobs are created, while missing entries remain `review` rather than being
  invented;
- representative-edit orchestration through the same v0.2 closed loop;
- an explicit-confirmation propagation executor with a checkpoint and read-back
  for every target, failure isolation, and no blind retry after uncertain state.
